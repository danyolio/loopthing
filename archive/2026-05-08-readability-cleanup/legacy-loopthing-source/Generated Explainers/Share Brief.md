# Share Brief

LoopThing is for the moment when another person or AI session needs to inherit your work without reading the whole transcript.

Send a `.loopthing` when you want a collaborator, teammate, fresh AI session, or future self to understand:

- what the project is trying to do
- what changed
- what was discarded and why
- what risks remain
- what happens next
- what context a fresh AI session should preserve

The emotional promise is simple:

```text
You do not have to start from scratch.
Here is the compressed state of the work.
```
