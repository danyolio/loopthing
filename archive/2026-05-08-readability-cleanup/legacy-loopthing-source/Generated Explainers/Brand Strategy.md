# Brand Strategy

## Brand Job

Make messy AI thinking feel trustworthy enough to send.

LoopThing should not look like an AI dashboard. It should feel like a reasoning dossier: editorial, exact, private, portable, and inspectable.

## Trust Promise

LoopThing compresses the mess without hiding judgment. It preserves what shifted, what died, why it died, what survived, and what happens next.

## Visual Stance

Use:

- field notebook
- evidence packet
- editorial dossier
- portable file object
- whiteboard lineage map

Avoid:

- AI gradient dashboard
- generic network graph
- purple glow
- "second brain" notebook cosplay
- corporate knowledge base

## Refero-Inspired Workflow

Use the Refero method as a discipline: extract taste into concrete rules an agent can follow. LoopThing's `DESIGN.md` should define colors, type, layout, components, motion, screenshot rules, and anti-patterns.

## Brand Line

Your best thinking, compressed.
