# 30-Day Plan

LoopThing v0 is a local compression harness for AI reasoning handoff.

## Week 1

Collect 20 real conversations and generate three variants for each: generic, structured, dual-render.

## Week 2

Run creator faithfulness and recipient comprehension scoring.

## Week 3

If signal exists, build the thin CLI:

- `loopthing compress`
- `loopthing score`
- `loopthing compare`
- `loopthing seal`

## Week 4

Publish anonymized results, share the harness, and decide whether the name should change before broader launch.

## Kill Criteria

Stop or pivot if structured artifacts do not beat generic summaries, recipients cannot recover the decision path, or creators do not want to send artifacts to real people.
