# Distribution Wedge

Start with a private compression challenge for AI-native builders.

## Challenge

Bring one messy AI conversation. Run:

- generic summary
- structured LoopThing compression
- dual-render handoff

Then score whether a recipient can understand the decision path in under five minutes.

## Why This Wins

It creates evidence before audience. It tests behavior instead of collecting compliments.

## After Signal

Open-source the harness with anonymized before/after examples. Publish the thesis post after the test, not before.
