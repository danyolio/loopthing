# Wedge Persona

Pinned persona: AI-native technical founders and product builders who need to hand off messy AI-assisted reasoning to a cofounder, teammate, or future self.

## Why This Persona

They already have the raw material: long AI conversations across coding, product, strategy, and writing. They already feel the pain: the value is in there, but they do not know where the gold is. They can also tolerate a CLI if it proves the artifact is useful.

## Killed Personas

- Consultants/agencies: too client-polish-driven for v1.
- Researchers/writers: plausible, but harder to score.
- General knowledge workers: too broad before the compression test.
- Pure engineering teams: useful later, but may collapse into ADR tooling.

## First Test

Use founder/builder chats where there is a real recipient: cofounder, collaborator, investor, teammate, or future self.
