# Artifact Contract

`reasoning.md` is the product nucleus.

Required sections:

- Intent
- Problem
- Critical messages
- Framing diffs
- Discarded branches
- What survives criticism
- Where the explanation might be wrong
- Outcome
- Next action
- Asks
- Meta

Optional supporting files:

- `source-metadata.json`
- `scorecard.md`
- `variants/`
- sealed `.loopthing`

## Principle

The `.loopthing` container is the seal step, not the first thing to prove. First prove that the compressed reasoning artifact is worth sending.
