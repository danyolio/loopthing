# Loop 10: Company, Brand, Business, Life Design

## Prompt

Ralph loop the company layer. Decide how LoopThing should design its brand, business model, MRR progression, life-design constraints, and operating system.

## Critique Of Previous Direction

Compression-first handoff is strategically sharper than viewer-first, but it is still mostly product truth. It does not yet answer:

- why someone should trust the brand
- how the company makes money
- whether the company can stay small
- how future loops should keep working across business, finance, marketing, design, website, product, and life design

## Regenerated Direction

LoopThing should be built as a **high-trust reasoning company**, not a generic AI SaaS.

The business wedge is compression-first handoff. The brand wedge is trust: make messy AI thinking feel like an artifact worth sending. The operating wedge is Ralph-style area loops, so every part of the company can be improved without losing lineage.

## Killed Branch

### Generic AI Dashboard Brand

Killed because it weakens the trust premise. If LoopThing looks like another vibe-coded dashboard, users will not believe it can compress important reasoning faithfully.

### Enterprise-First Services

Killed for now because it violates the life-design constraint and would turn LoopThing into consulting with a file extension.

### Brand As Surface Polish

Killed because brand is part of the product trust layer. The user is sending this artifact to someone else; visual trust changes whether the artifact is opened, believed, and forwarded.
