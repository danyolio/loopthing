# Loop 05: Wedge Persona

## Question

Who has the most urgent first use case for compression-first handoff?

## Branches

### Technical Founder / Product Builder

**Thesis:** The first user is an AI-native founder or product-minded builder using chat across strategy, writing, coding, and product decisions.

**Evidence:** The current project itself is exactly this pattern: many chat sessions across tools, high-value reasoning, repeated reframing, and a need to hand the result to a cofounder or collaborator.

**Critique:** This persona may be too close to the creator. Risk of solving a personal workflow and mistaking it for a market.

**Regenerated:** Start here anyway because the feedback loop is fastest and the chats are available this weekend.

**Verdict:** Pinned for the test.

### Engineering Lead / Architect

**Thesis:** The first user is an engineering lead compressing architecture conversations into decision records.

**Evidence:** Technical decisions have real handoff cost and clear discarded branches.

**Critique:** This may collapse into ADRs, PRDs, or GitHub issue summaries. The wedge is narrower but less novel.

**Verdict:** Survivor, likely second test domain.

### Researcher / Writer

**Thesis:** The first user is a researcher or writer turning long exploratory sessions into argument lineage.

**Evidence:** Reasoning traces matter, and compression could preserve intellectual moves.

**Critique:** Harder to score. "Good writing" and "good reasoning handoff" blur together.

**Verdict:** Hold, not v1.

### Consultant / Agency

**Thesis:** The first user is someone handing client work back with visible reasoning.

**Evidence:** Handoff is frequent and client-facing.

**Critique:** Social risk: clients may not want to see AI process, and polished deliverables still dominate.

**Verdict:** Killed for v1.

## Pinned Persona

AI-native technical founders and product builders who need to hand off messy AI-assisted reasoning to a cofounder, teammate, or future self.

## Why This Wins

They have the raw material, the pain, the willingness to use a CLI, and high-value handoff moments. Most importantly, they can run the compression test immediately.
