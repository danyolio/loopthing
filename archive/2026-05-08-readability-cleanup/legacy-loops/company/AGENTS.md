# LoopThing Company Agents

## Operating Principle

LoopThing is compression-first handoff software. Every company loop should improve one of these:

- the quality of compressed reasoning artifacts
- the trustworthiness of the brand
- the clarity of the business wedge
- the probability of reaching revenue without building a support-heavy company

## Current Pinned Direction

LoopThing v0 is a local compression harness for AI-native founders and product builders. The first proof is whether a recipient can read a compressed artifact in under five minutes and land where the creator landed.

## Brand North Star

Do not make LoopThing look like an AI dashboard. Make it feel like a trustworthy reasoning object: editorial, exact, private, legible, and worth sending.

## Life Design Constraint

The company should be designed so a small team can do deep work and still live. Prefer self-serve onboarding, async support, local-first defaults, and pricing that avoids custom-services gravity.
