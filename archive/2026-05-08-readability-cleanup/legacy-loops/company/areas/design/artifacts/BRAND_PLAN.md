# Brand Plan

## Brand Job

LoopThing needs to make messy AI thinking feel trustworthy enough to send.

The brand should not say "look at our AI." It should say: **this is a serious reasoning artifact**.

## Trust Promise

LoopThing compresses the mess without hiding the judgment. It preserves the moves that matter: what shifted, what died, why it died, what survived, and what someone should do next.

## Visual Positioning

Not:

- AI dashboard
- neon graph toy
- generic SaaS gradient
- "second brain" notebook cosplay
- corporate knowledge base

Yes:

- field notebook
- evidence packet
- editorial dossier
- portable file object
- whiteboard lineage map
- quiet technical tool

## Taste Direction

Use Refero as a working method, not a mood board. Refero's useful premise is that taste can be extracted into colors, type, spacing, components, and a `DESIGN.md` an agent can use.

LoopThing's own `DESIGN.md` should be the same: executable taste. No vague "premium, clean, modern" language.

## Tone

Plain, exact, and a little sharp.

Good lines:

- "Find the gold in the chat."
- "The reasoning ships too."
- "Not a transcript. Not a summary. A handoff."
- "Your best thinking, compressed."

Avoid:

- "unlock insights"
- "seamless collaboration"
- "AI-powered knowledge management"
- "revolutionize your workflow"

## Trust Signals

- show message counts and source shape
- show exact critical messages
- show discarded branches with reasons
- show what was inferred vs quoted
- show privacy/local-first defaults
- make the file object visible

## Website Feel

First screen should be an artifact, not a hero illustration. The viewer/file should be visible immediately with an example before/after:

- source material: 100 messages across 86 files
- LoopThing: 1 reasoning artifact, 1 agent handoff, 6 discarded branches, 3 asks

## Brand Thesis

LoopThing wins trust by making the creator look clearer without pretending the process was cleaner than it was.
