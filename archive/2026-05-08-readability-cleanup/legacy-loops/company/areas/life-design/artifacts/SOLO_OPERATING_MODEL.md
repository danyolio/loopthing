# Solo Operating Model

## Direct Answer

Yes, LoopThing can be run completely solo through the proof stage and probably to $10k MRR.

It can maybe reach $100k MRR solo if the product is self-serve, local-first, support-light, and narrow.

It should not be planned as a completely solo $1M MRR company unless the founder is willing to avoid enterprise services, use contractors, and accept slower growth.

## Life Design Thesis

"Let my people go surfing" is not a vibe pasted onto the brand. It is an operating constraint:

- low meeting surface
- async support
- local-first product
- no custom deployments before strong pull
- high prices relative to support load
- evidence-based roadmap
- weekly deep-work cycles

## Weekly Cadence

Monday: product loop and customer evidence.

Tuesday-Wednesday: build only.

Thursday: compression challenge calls or async reviews.

Friday: ship artifact, write progress, publish proof.

Surf/life blocks are protected. If the company cannot respect them at small scale, the product is too service-heavy.

## Hiring Gates

Do not hire for ego. Hire only when a constraint breaks:

- $10k MRR: contractors only for design, docs, screenshots, bookkeeping.
- $100k MRR: part-time support/ops and one product engineer if support interrupts build time.
- $1M MRR: small team required unless growth is entirely self-serve.

## Company Shape

Best shape: tiny, profitable, high-trust software company with an open local core and paid team handoff layer.

Worst shape: AI consulting company with a file extension.
