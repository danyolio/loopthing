# LoopThing Business Plan

## One-Line Business

LoopThing turns messy AI chat sessions into compressed reasoning artifacts that make handoff faster, smarter, and less embarrassing.

## Wedge

The first wedge is **AI-native founders and product builders handing off messy reasoning to a cofounder, teammate, investor, or future self**.

They already have the behavior: they copy-paste old chats into new chats because they know the gold is in there. The current solution is bad because it grabs everything and makes the reader dig.

## Product

Start with a local compression harness:

- import one or more AI chat transcripts
- extract critical messages, framing diffs, discarded branches, risks, decisions, next actions, and asks
- produce `reasoning.md`
- score creator faithfulness and recipient comprehension
- seal useful outputs into `yourproject.loopthing`

The viewer, brand, slides, media, and rich artifacts matter only after compression quality works.

## Customer

Initial customer: high-agency AI users whose work is exploratory and multiplayer.

Best early persona: technical founders, indie hackers, product leads, strategists, and design engineers who use AI to think through product decisions, then need someone else to understand the decision path.

## Market

The market is not "AI notes." It is the emerging category of **reasoning handoff** for AI work.

Current alternatives are weak:

- raw chat transcripts are noisy
- summaries flatten the decision path
- docs describe the conclusion, not the reasoning
- memory products are single-player and lock-in oriented
- project management tools store tasks, not thought movement

## Pricing

- Free: open local CLI, one-off compression, local files
- Pro: $29-49/month, unlimited personal compressions, scoring, redaction, container sealing, private library
- Team: $15-25/seat/month or $199-499/workspace/month, shared handoff library, comments, permissions, templates
- Studio/Founding Teams: $1,000-3,000/month for early design partners only, capped and time-boxed

## Moat

The moat is not the zip format. The moat is compression quality plus artifact taste:

- strong schemas for reasoning compression
- benchmark corpus of before/after handoffs
- trust in the artifact as something worth sending
- templates by work type: product decision, strategy, research, writing, code exploration
- eventually, a network of reusable reasoning artifacts

## Go-To-Market

Start with a private compression challenge:

1. Bring one messy AI conversation.
2. Generate generic summary, structured LoopThing, and dual-render handoff.
3. Give the artifact to someone who was not in the original conversation.
4. Score whether they land where the creator landed in under five minutes.

Publish anonymized before/after artifacts only after the results are real.

## Core Risk

If compression quality is mediocre, everything else is theater. The business should not scale until the compression test passes.

## Next Proof

Run 20 real conversations this weekend. If recipients reliably understand the decision path faster than summaries, build the CLI. If not, improve the prompt and schema before touching SaaS.
