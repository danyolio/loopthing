# MRR Ladder

## Principle

Revenue targets should not drag LoopThing into a services company. Every milestone needs a product-led path and a support-load constraint.

## $10k MRR

**Goal:** prove people will pay for reasoning compression.

Plausible paths:

- 250 Pro users at $40/month
- 50 team workspaces at $199/month
- 10 founding teams at $1,000/month for a capped design-partner program

Best path: a mixed early wedge.

- 10 founding teams x $1,000 = $10k MRR
- Cap the program at 10.
- Give them direct access, but sell the artifact outcome, not consulting hours.

Solo feasibility: very doable if onboarding is batched and support is async.

## $100k MRR

**Goal:** turn proof into repeatable product-led sales.

Plausible path:

- 1,000 Pro users x $39 = $39k
- 250 team workspaces x $249 = $62.25k
- Total: about $101k MRR

Required product changes:

- importers for ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, Cursor, and plain markdown
- redaction and local-first privacy story
- team handoff library
- examples that make artifacts feel worth sending

Solo feasibility: possible but hard. Use contractors for support/docs/design. Do not add custom enterprise deployments.

## $1M MRR

**Goal:** category-level company, not a solo utility.

Plausible paths:

- 2,000 team workspaces x $500 = $1M MRR
- 20,000 Pro users x $50 = $1M MRR
- 500 larger orgs x $2,000 = $1M MRR

Best path if the category works: teams and orgs, not solo subscriptions only.

Required product changes:

- shared reasoning libraries
- policy and redaction controls
- artifact permissions
- workflow integrations
- organization-level templates
- trust/compliance posture

Solo feasibility: not honestly, unless the product is wildly self-serve and support-light. A solo founder can reach the proof and maybe $100k MRR. At $1M MRR, plan for a tiny elite team or a contractor network.

## Kill Criteria

Do not chase $1M MRR if it forces:

- custom services as the main revenue
- enterprise procurement before product pull
- always-on support
- opaque hosted memory as the core product
