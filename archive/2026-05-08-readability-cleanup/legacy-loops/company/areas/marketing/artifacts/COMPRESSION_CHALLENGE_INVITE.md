# Private Compression Challenge Invite

## Short DM

I am testing LoopThing, a tool that turns messy AI chats into a five-minute reasoning handoff.

The question is simple: can someone else understand where your thinking landed without reading the whole transcript?

Would you bring one real messy AI conversation from product, strategy, writing, or code? I will run it through LoopThing, give you the compressed artifact, and then we test whether a recipient can understand the decision path in five minutes.

Time ask: 20 minutes from you, plus one person you trust to read the artifact.

## Who Qualifies

- Uses AI for real exploratory work
- Has at least one messy but valuable chat
- Has someone they might actually send the artifact to
- Is willing to say whether the artifact is sendable or not

## The Ask

Send:

- one exported chat or pasted transcript
- one sentence on what the chat was about
- who the recipient would be

I will return:

- `reasoning.md`
- source metadata
- score checklist
- optional sealed `.loopthing`

## The Hook

Do not bring your cleanest chat. Bring the one where you know there is gold, but you would never forward the transcript.
