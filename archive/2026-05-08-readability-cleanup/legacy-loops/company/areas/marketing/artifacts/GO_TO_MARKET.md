# Go-To-Market Plan

## Principle

Do not launch with a category claim first. Launch with proof that LoopThing finds the gold in messy chats.

## First Audience

AI-native founders, product builders, indie hackers, strategists, design engineers, and technical writers who already use AI for exploratory work.

## First Offer

"Bring one messy AI conversation. We will turn it into a five-minute reasoning handoff and test whether another person lands where you landed."

## First 50 Users

1. 10 founder friends with real messy chats
2. 10 AI builders from hackathon/tech circles
3. 10 design/product operators
4. 10 technical writers/researchers
5. 10 people who regularly send long context dumps to collaborators

## Channels

- private DMs
- founder communities
- AI builder Discords/Slacks
- one public teardown post
- one short video showing raw chat to reasoning artifact

## Proof Assets

- anonymized before/after compression examples
- recipient comprehension scores
- "critical messages found" examples
- discarded-branch examples
- a tiny public corpus of excellent LoopThings

## Launch Sequence

Week 1: private compression challenge.

Week 2: publish results and artifacts.

Week 3: release local CLI alpha.

Week 4: invite paid founding teams.

## What Not To Do

- Do not sell as memory.
- Do not lead with the file format.
- Do not overbuild the viewer.
- Do not ask people if they like the idea; ask them to bring a chat.
