## One-Liner

LoopThing compresses messy AI work into a handoff artifact for the next chat, agent, collaborator, or future self.

## Short Explanation

Raw chats keep everything. Summaries flatten everything. LoopThing extracts the load-bearing reasoning: decisions, direction, critical turns, discarded paths, risks, asks, and next actions.

The most important output is `agent-handoff.md`: paste-ready context that lets a fresh AI session continue from the real state of the work.
