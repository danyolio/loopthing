# Share Brief

LoopThing is for the moment when you want to show someone not only what you made, but how smart the path was.

Send a `.loopthing` when you want a friend, collaborator, coworker, judge, teacher, or future teammate to understand:

- the original idea
- the turns it took
- the discarded directions
- the generated explainers
- the media, visuals, and screenshots
- the docs, slides, prototype, and code
- why the final version is stronger than the first one

The emotional promise is simple:

```text
I did not just prompt until something happened.
I shaped an idea, made decisions, and packaged the thinking so you can see it.
```
